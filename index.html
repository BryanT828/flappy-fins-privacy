Privacy Policy for Flappy Fins!

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

We operate the Flappy Fins! mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information across all worldwide regions of distribution, in compliance with GDPR, CalOPPA, CCPA, and COPPA standards.

1. Personal Information & Data Collection

Flappy Fins! does not require user account registration, login credentials, or personal contact details. All core game progress—including unlocked aquatic characters, earned shell currency, and local high scores—is stored exclusively on your local device storage.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

We monetize Flappy Fins! using Google AdMob to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. To serve ads, Google AdMob may automatically process non-personal advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), coarse location data, IP address, and device metadata.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, please visit Google Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Flappy Fins! is an all-ages arcade game designed for global audiences, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Third-party advertising requests served via Google AdMob are configured to comply with applicable children’s privacy standards.

4. Worldwide Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you are residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, California, or other jurisdiction with data privacy laws:

  • You have the right to request information regarding data processed by third-party ad networks.
  • You can manage or reset your advertising identifier via your device settings (iOS App Tracking Transparency or Android Advertising Settings).
